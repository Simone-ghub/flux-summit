Partners

More joining
innovUp
Ecosystem association
Read bio+

InnovUp is the Italian association representing the innovation and startup ecosystem, born from the merger of Italia Startup and APSTI. It unites startups, scale-ups, innovation centers, incubators and accelerators across Italy and Europe, working on advocacy, networking and knowledge sharing as Italy's representative to IASP, the European Startup Network and EBAN.

innovup.net →
ELIS Innovation Hub
Open innovation
Read bio+

ELIS Innovation Hub is a structure within the ELIS consortium that fosters open innovation by connecting universities, research centers and startups with corporate needs. Through its OPEN ITALY co-creation program it has supported 58 corporate clients and a network of 542 startups, generating 134 projects since 2017.

elisinnovationhub.com →
Roma Startup
Action think tank
Read bio+

Roma Startup is an action think tank founded in 2012, working with public and private actors to turn Rome into a leading Mediterranean hub for entrepreneurial talent, technology and investment. It runs Rome Startup Week, the Angels of Rome investment community and the Allied4Startups global coalition.

romastartup.org →
Peekaboo
Startup studio & venture builder
Read bio+

Peekaboo is an Italian company supporting youth entrepreneurship by connecting young people's original ideas with corporations open to innovation. It acts as a bridge between aspiring founders and established companies looking for fresh perspectives.

peekaboovision.com →
Scuter
Mobility partner
Read bio+

Scuter is an Italian electric three-wheeled sharing mobility platform for cities, businesses and territories, combining vehicles, software and fleet operations into one system. As FLUX's Mobility Partner, Scuter supports getting attendees around during the event.

scuter.co →
Hi!Founders
Startup community
Read bio+

Hi!Founders is Italy's largest early-stage startup community, an almost 4,000-member Telegram community built by touring the Italian startup ecosystem for nearly three years. It connects founders, operators and ecosystem players across the country, and its network is central to how FLUX reaches early-stage founders nationwide.

FAQ

Quick answers
Are sessions recorded or streamed?+
No. The 5 November sessions are in-room only. Some side events run online instead.
Do I register separately for side events?+
Yes. Each one has its own sign-up. Some are in person in Rome, some online.
What does it cost?+
The 5 November main event is free. The Executive Retreat (6–8 Nov, Itri) and the Executive Partner Lunch (5 Nov) are paid, open to anyone who books. Price and booking for both are on Luma, not listed here.
How do I sponsor or partner?+
Fill the sponsor form on the site. The deck arrives by email within a minute.
How do I reach the organizers?+
Write to simonemartinelli95@gmail.com or join the Telegram group linked in the header.
